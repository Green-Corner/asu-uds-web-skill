P — global footer (not a Pitchfork block — visual approximation)

Required on every page. Three real compositions from the same building blocks — pick by how much a unit actually has to link to, not by habit. Full: brand row plus however many columns the unit needs. Collapsed: brand row only, no columns — for a unit with nothing to put in them. Minimal: no brand row at all — a sub-page section that just needs the legal/utility bar.
